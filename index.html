Pressio

Your blood pressure diary

Pressio is a simple diary for your home blood pressure monitor. Write down readings in seconds, see your morning and evening averages, and bring a clear PDF report to your doctor.

  • Big keypad that jumps from SYS to DIA to PUL by itself
  • Zones by the ESH 2023 or AHA/ACC 2017 table — color, word and icon
  • Charts, averages and a report for your doctor
  • Morning and evening reminders, medications, family profiles
  • Your readings stay on your phone. No account, no ads

Pressio does not measure blood pressure: use a validated blood pressure monitor. Pressio is not a medical device and does not replace your doctor.

Available for iPhone and Android.
