How do I keep my readings when I change phones?

Turn on “Save readings to Apple Health” (iPhone) or “Save readings to Health Connect” (Android) in Settings, or save a backup file (Settings → Data → Save backup file). On the new phone, use “Import from Apple Health / Health Connect” or “Restore from backup file”.

I deleted a reading by mistake

Open Settings → Data → Recently deleted. Deleted readings are kept there for 30 days.

How do I send my readings to my doctor?

Settings → Data → Report for your doctor creates a PDF with averages, zones, a chart and all readings. You can also export a CSV table for Excel.

How do I cancel Pressio Pro?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pressio. On Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Pressio. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Pressio Pro and tap “Restore purchases”.

Reminders don’t come

Check that notifications for Pressio are allowed in the phone’s settings. On Android, also allow “Alarms & reminders” for exact times; otherwise a reminder may arrive up to 10 minutes late.
